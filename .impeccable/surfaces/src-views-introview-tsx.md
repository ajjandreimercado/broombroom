Scope: whole Groom Broom web app (landing = Persuade; marketplace, detail, wizard, account, seller/admin dashboards = Operate inside the same world). Visitor: PH buyer choosing a vehicle and applying; sellers/admins operating listings.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The site is a dealer service booklet: ruled paper, carbon ink, and one orange rubber stamp that marks what you have done. It refuses the dark car-photo hero with a floating search pill and the grid of shadowed rounded cards.

OWN-WORLD: Cool bond-paper white (#F7F8F8) ground, carbon ink (#141517), blue-grey ruling (#C9D1D6 hairline, #8C989F strong), logo blue #1D5FD1 for primary actions and logo green #1B7F35 as stamp ink for stamped/active states (palette changed 2026-10-08 with the new Broom Broom logo; previously orange #E8541A). Two inks, no gradients, no decorative shadows. Listings are ruled records: photo plate, then specs in ruled cells with tabular figures. Archivo (width axis): Expanded/SemiExpanded for display, normal width for UI; tabular numerals for price, km, dates. Corners square (2px max).

STORY: A buyer sees one real vehicle and a working search in the first viewport, understands every vehicle type is here with an in-site application, browses ruled records, stamps the ones they like, and applies.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Left 7/12: large expanded headline, then the search set as a ruled form row (Looking for / Budget up to / keyword) with an blue "Search" button flush right of the row. Right 5/12: one full-height vehicle photo plate with its record strip beneath (title, ₱ price, location, km) in ruled cells. Category index as a ruled row with counts across the bottom of the viewport.

FORM: Casa PMS service booklet, position 6 of my ordered list, seed key dec73a51. Raises: no boxed cards, rules only (cracktro); line weight encodes state (event display); strictly two inks, no shadows (noir one-sheet); orange only on the active/stamped item (film strip); the shell never moves between views (PC-98). Signature interaction: the stamp press on save/compare/apply/step-complete (scale 1.6->1, rotate -8deg, ink opacity in, 220ms ease-out).

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
